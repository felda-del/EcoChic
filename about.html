About Us

Why EcoChic Exists

EcoChic was created as a sustainability and fashion concept that makes lower-impact living easier to understand. Instead of only describing the problem, the project focuses on actions people can take right away.

The site combines guidance, self-assessment, product comparison, and progress tracking to show that sustainable fashion can be thoughtful, realistic, and accessible.

Our Mission: Making Sustainability Practical

EcoChic is designed around three ideas that are easy to explain in a portfolio or interview:

  1. Education - Explain sustainable fashion in clear, concrete language.
  2. Action - Turn ideas into tools people can actually use.
  3. Consistency - Encourage repeatable habits over one-time perfect choices.

Design Philosophy

The project stays intentionally simple: no extra frameworks, no heavy dependencies, and no complex back end. That keeps the code approachable while still demonstrating strong front-end structure, local storage, validation, filtering logic, and interactive features.

How the Experience Helps Users

Someone visiting EcoChic can compare lower-impact options, save favorites, get tailored style guidance, and measure their current habits. The result is a site that feels more like a practical sustainability companion than a static information page.

Better fashion habits start with better information and smaller, repeatable choices.
