    Quick FAQ

    What makes a fashion choice more sustainable?

    Usually it is a mix of buying less often, choosing better materials, rewearing items longer, and caring for garments well.

    Does sustainable always mean expensive?

    No. Some of the strongest habits are low-cost, such as buying second-hand, repairing clothes, and avoiding impulse purchases.

    Why does EcoChic focus on realistic habits?

    Because repeated small decisions are easier to keep and often matter more than one perfect purchase.
