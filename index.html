Welcome to EcoChic

The way we dress, shop, and care for our clothes shapes our environmental footprint. EcoChic helps people make more sustainable fashion choices through simple guidance, useful planning tools, and habits that can actually stick.

Why This Project Matters

Sustainable fashion is often discussed in abstract terms, yet most people are looking for concrete next steps. EcoChic bridges that gap by combining education, product comparison, wardrobe care, and behavior tracking into one easy-to-follow experience.

The goal is not perfection. It is to help someone buy less impulsively, rewear more intentionally, and understand how small changes can add up over time.

What You Can Do Here

  • Browse a product explorer with sustainability filters, search, sort, and saved favorites.
  • Take a style quiz to get realistic recommendations based on habits and budget.
  • Track weekly progress and calculate a simple sustainability score for your wardrobe routine.
  • Read curated learning resources about sustainable materials, shopping habits, and fashion systems.

Start With One Better Choice

Whether that means repairing a shirt, buying second-hand denim, washing clothes in cold water, or pausing before a purchase, the best sustainability habit is the one you can keep repeating. EcoChic is built to make those choices easier to understand.
